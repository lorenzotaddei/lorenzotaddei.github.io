scART for Play

A digital project that transforms food packaging waste into simple play activities for children.
It aims to make reuse creative, accessible, and easy to apply in everyday contexts.

How it works & Prototype

The app generates customized play activities based on a few simple inputs:

  • Materials available → food packaging waste at hand
  • Child’s age → to adapt complexity and interaction
  • Tools available → common items found at home

Based on these elements, the app suggests simple, creative, and easy-to-follow play activities.
